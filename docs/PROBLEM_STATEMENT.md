# Problem Statement

## YOUPER — University Student Mental Health Support Platform

### The Problem

University students face unprecedented mental health challenges, including stress, anxiety, depression, and social isolation — yet many lack timely access to counseling resources. Traditional campus mental health services often have:

- Long wait times for counselor appointments
- Limited availability (office hours during class times)
- Stigma that prevents students from seeking help in person
- Fragmented resources scattered across different websites and services
- No mechanism for peer support or community learning from shared experiences
- Lack of self-awareness about mood patterns and emotional triggers

Additionally, students need just-in-time support — accessible via mobile devices, available 24/7, and offering immediate coping strategies without requiring a full counseling session.

### Current Gaps

1. No centralized mental health hub for students — information is fragmented across multiple services
2. Limited emotional insight tools — students can't easily track and understand mood patterns
3. Isolation from peer perspectives — no safe space to share concerns and learn from similar experiences
4. Barrier to professional help — no easy path to schedule counselors or access emergency support
5. No educational resources — students lack practical guidance on mindfulness, stress management, and anxiety reduction

### The Solution: YOUPER

A mobile-first web application that provides university students with:

- **Mood tracking & analytics** — daily mood logging with visual trend analysis
- **Educational library** — self-guided mental health resources (mindfulness, anxiety, stress, depression management)
- **Anonymous community forum** — peer discussion and shared experiences without stigma
- **Direct counselor access** — one-click scheduling and emergency support hotlines
- **Dark mode & offline persistence** — campus-friendly design that works in any lighting and stores data locally

### Who Benefits

- Students seeking mental health support, coping strategies, and peer validation
- Campus counseling centers, to triage demand and provide asynchronous support
- Peer support programs, with structured resources and community engagement
- Mental health advocates promoting awareness and early intervention

### Success Criteria

- Students can track mood patterns over weeks and months
- Emergency support contacts are one tap away
- Counselors can be scheduled without calling during office hours
- Peer discussions offer alternative perspectives before/instead of formal counseling
- Educational library covers the top 6 mental health topics for students (mindfulness, anxiety, stress, depression, meditation, overwhelm)
